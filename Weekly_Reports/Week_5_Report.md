1. Worked on a New Dataset
Dataset Used: HR Employee Attrition Dataset

Dataset Description
The HR Employee Attrition Dataset contains employee demographic, professional and workplace-related information. The primary objective of the dataset is to analyze employee characteristics and predict whether an employee is likely to leave the organization (Attrition). The dataset contains 1,470 employee records with 35 features including both numerical and categorical attributes, making it suitable for data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, feature engineering and machine learning applications.

Some important features include:
Age
Business Travel
Department
Education
Job Role
Monthly Income
Job Satisfaction
Work-Life Balance
Years at Company
Overtime
Attrition (Target Variable)
