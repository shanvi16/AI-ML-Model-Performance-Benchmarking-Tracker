Overview
During Week 6, I focused on designing and developing three interactive Power BI dashboards based on the Adult Census Income dataset. The objective was to present meaningful insights through well-structured visualizations and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs). The dashboards were designed following Power BI best practices to provide a clean, interactive and user-friendly experience for analyzing demographic, educational, employment and financial trends within the dataset.

Tasks Completed

1. Dashboard 1 – Population & Income Analysis
Created the first dashboard to provide an overview of the dataset and income distribution.

KPIs Included
Total Records
Average Age
High Income Population (>50K)
Average Working Hours

Visualizations
Age Distribution Histogram
Income by Gender
Income Distribution (Donut Chart)
The dashboard provides insights into the overall population, age trends, gender-wise income distribution and the proportion of individuals earning above and below $50K.

2. Dashboard 2 – Education & Employment Analysis
Developed the second dashboard to analyze education and employment characteristics.

KPIs Included
Education Levels
Occupations
Workclasses
Marital Status Categories

Visualizations
Education Level Distribution
Occupation Distribution
Workclass Distribution
This dashboard highlights the educational background, employment sectors, workclass categories and marital status of individuals within the dataset.

3. Dashboard 3 – Demographic & Financial Analysis
Created the third dashboard to analyze financial and demographic information.

KPIs Included
Average Capital Gain
Average Capital Loss
Number of Native Countries

Visualizations
Native Country Distribution
Race Distribution
Average Capital Gain by Income Category
This dashboard provides insights into financial indicators, country-wise population distribution, race distribution and income-related capital gains.

4. Dashboard Design and Implementation
Designed three interactive dashboards in Microsoft Power BI.
Used KPI Cards, Bar Charts, Column Charts, Donut Charts and Histograms.
Applied consistent color themes and clean layouts.
Added interactive filters and slicers to improve data exploration.
Organized visuals to maintain readability and effective visual hierarchy.

5. Dashboard Validation
Verified the accuracy of KPI calculations.
Tested visual interactions and dashboard responsiveness.
Reviewed layouts to ensure clarity and consistency.
Ensured all dashboards effectively communicated business insights.

Key Learnings
Designing professional dashboards using Microsoft Power BI.
Organizing KPIs into meaningful dashboard sections.
Selecting appropriate visualizations for different types of data.
Applying dashboard design principles such as visual hierarchy and consistency.
Creating interactive reports using filters, slicers and KPI cards.

Outcome
By the end of Week 6, I successfully developed three interactive Power BI dashboards that visualized key insights from the Adult Census Income dataset. The dashboards presented demographic, educational, employment, and financial information through clear KPIs and interactive visualizations. This enhanced my practical skills in business intelligence, dashboard design and data visualization while supporting the objectives of the AI/ML Model Performance Benchmarking Tracker project
